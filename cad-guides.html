Learning path: from the first plan to a presentation

Start by choosing meters or millimeters and drafting a rectangular room with known dimensions. Use object snaps to close the corners, separate elements into layers and add dimensions. Then place doors, windows and furniture from the library and inspect their actual sizes in Properties.

Save an editable version before exporting a test DXF. Reopen it and measure a known distance to check the scale. To prepare a sheet, set the paper size and plotting scale without changing model-space dimensions. If you need to study massing, try the 3D workspace with walls and slabs; for concept communication, explore artistic rendering. The guides on this page address the individual stages of that workflow.

Downloadable CAD resources: original DXF exercises

Four Blackline teaching files for practicing editable geometry in meters. Open CAD2D3D, import the DXF and check drawing units. These are teaching exercises, not construction documents; use is subject to the current terms.

  • Download a 4 × 5 m room (DXF): 20 m² reference geometry, perimeter lines, a 0.90 m door opening and swing arc, a 1.60 m window opening, and construction axes on separate layers. Schematic representation without construction wall thickness.
  • Download the 1:1 scale test (DXF): 10 m and 4 m lines and a circle with a 1 m radius. Verify lengths with DIST.
  • Straight stair: plan and section (DXF): 16 risers of 0.175 m, 15 treads of 0.28 m, total rise 2.80 m and horizontal run 4.20 m. Schematic width 1.10 m. Read the exercise.
  • Wall with door opening (DXF): 5 m length, 0.20 m thickness and schematic 0.90 m opening on separate layers. Read the exercise.

Generate floor plans from architectural instructions

ARQASSIST interprets quantities and dimensions to generate editable CAD geometry. Try bedrooms x 5 of 4 by 5 m, swimming pool 8 by 4 m, parking x 8 of 2.5 by 5 m or classroom 8 by 9 m for 30 students. It also supports public restroom layouts, auditoriums and furnished dining rooms. These layouts are conceptual proposals, not regulatory compliance checks.

Calibrate architectural reference images

After importing a JPG or PNG floor plan, verify its scale before tracing. A known dimension is the preferred reference; experimental toilet-based estimation with WCSCALE or ESCALAINODORO only gives an approximate scale. Confirm the total image width and measure an independent known distance. Local visual recognition may require an initial model download.

CAD drawing quality checklist

Before delivering a drawing, verify a known length, coincident endpoints using object snaps (OSNAP), closed contours and the absence of duplicate lines. Check that dimensions match the geometry and that layers distinguish walls, openings, furniture and annotations.

Keep the original file and an editable copy; verify the plot scale and reopen the exported DXF to validate its geometry. For DWG files, review the limitations of local conversion. To practice precision drafting, follow the technical drawing guide for students.
