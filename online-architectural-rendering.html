Online architectural rendering from a 3D CAD model

The Artistic Render studio in CAD2D3D by Blackline turns an architectural model view into an illustration. It is designed for concept presentations, massing studies and architectural communication without redrawing model geometry.

Launch CAD2D3D

How to create an artistic architectural render

  1. Launch CAD2D3D and prepare a model with walls, slabs and materials.
  2. In the 3D workspace, adjust the camera and lighting to compose the view.
  3. In the camera toolbar, select Artistic render. The studio captures the current view and lets you compare it with the finished illustration.
  4. Choose a visual style and adjust line weight, hatching, shadows and paper color. Refresh the capture if the camera changes.
  5. Export to PNG, JPG or raster PDF and check the resolution for your presentation.

How does this differ from photorealistic rendering?

An artistic sketch emphasizes composition, spatial legibility and graphic character rather than reproducing a photograph. The engine uses geometry, depth, normals, materials and lighting passes alongside stroke, paper and pigment effects. It does not alter the model or replace scaled technical drawings.

Do I need to install software?

Local rendering runs in the browser inside the CAD2D3D 3D workspace. Resolution and speed depend on the device and model. Pro tools, including optional AI retouching when available, are subject to the current plan shown in the editor.

How do I prepare a presentation sheet?

Save your editable project separately. Export a PNG or JPG for page layout, or a raster PDF for illustration. For dimensioned plans and construction documentation, use the technical drafting and scaled plotting workflow.
