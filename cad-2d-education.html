BLACKLINE CAD · FOR EDUCATION

Online 2D CAD for architecture students and teachers

Blackline CAD is an independent browser-based drafting application developed by an architecture studio in Buenos Aires. It offers a practical environment for introducing lines, layers, dimensions, object snaps and technical drawing workflows without installing a desktop CAD application.

A 30-minute classroom drawing exercise

  1. Choose drawing units and draw a simple rectangular room using dimensions set by the instructor.
  2. Add a partition and test line, polyline and object snapping tools.
  3. Organize geometry in layers and add basic dimensions.
  4. Export the drawing to DXF or PDF, then verify the result and measured lengths.

This activity is a suggested exercise, not a certified curriculum or an official course. See the beginner's 2D CAD guide and DXF compatibility notes.

Precision drafting exercise: object snaps and tracking

In a test drawing, draft a 4 × 5 m room and enable object snaps (OSNAP) and object snap tracking (OTRACK). Acquire a vertex, move the cursor toward the alignment and verify that the new line endpoint matches the reference. Repeat with a polyline and with ORTHO enabled and disabled. Assess coordinates, parallelism, dimensions and connected endpoints.

For a second exercise, separate walls, openings, furniture and dimensions into layers. Export DXF and PDF and verify the plotting scale. Consult the architectural drafting guides and the introduction to 3D modeling.

Teaching worksheet: drawing exercise and assessment

Suggested assessment out of 100 points: geometric accuracy and units (30); connected geometry and layer organization (25); dimensions and legibility (20); DXF verification and plotting scale (25).

Deliverables: an editable drawing, a PDF at a declared scale, and a short note reporting the dimensional verification. Instructors can adapt the rubric and test compatibility before assigning graded work.

Who may find it useful?

Architecture and engineering students, technical drawing instructors, design schools and professionals who want to assess an alternative online 2D CAD workflow. The software is being actively developed; instructors should test required tools and file compatibility before assigning graded work.

Limitations and compatibility

Blackline CAD is independently developed and not affiliated with other CAD vendors. DXF support may not preserve every entity in complex drawings. Availability of DWG conversion can depend on configured services. Always check dimensions, annotations and exported files in a second CAD application when accuracy matters.

Invitation for educators

We welcome practical feedback from teaching teams about precision, command workflows, accessibility and classroom use. If you try a simple exercise, share what worked and what should be improved. There is no claim of university endorsement.
