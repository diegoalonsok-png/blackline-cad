Parametric door and window CAD blocks

Parametric openings let designers reuse a door or window definition while testing different sizes.

Launch CAD2D3D

Choose the right representation

Browse the library for plan, section or elevation symbols. A plan door should show its frame and swing where appropriate.

Adjust dimensions

For supported dynamic blocks, use available properties and grips to resize the opening. Some symbols preserve their aspect ratio; independent dimension editing is not available for every block.

Coordinate with walls

Verify rough openings, wall thickness and swing clearance. If the opening does not trim the wall automatically in your case, adjust the wall lines before dimensioning.

Worked example: resizing an architectural opening

Draw a test wall in model units with a nominal thickness of 0.20 m. Insert a supported parametric door from the gallery and select it to inspect its available properties. Test an opening width of 0.80 m and then 0.90 m on a duplicate instance. Measure both instances independently to confirm that the definition behaves as expected and that other references remain unchanged.

Inspect both wall faces, the jamb position and the door swing after resizing. For a window, distinguish the rough wall opening from the frame and glazing dimensions. Use a separate layer for opening annotations and identify sill and head levels on elevations or sections. Export a sample DXF and reopen it before relying on interchange: proprietary dynamic constraints from other CAD systems may not survive conversion. Example dimensions are for practice only.
