Bathroom CAD design and plumbing fixture blocks

Bathroom design coordinates room dimensions, fixtures, circulation and openings. A CAD plan helps compare layouts before specifying the installation.

Launch CAD2D3D

Prepare the floor plan

Draw the interior perimeter at real dimensions, separate walls and equipment into layers, and mark the door position. Allow enough clearance for its swing.

Place fixtures

Browse available plumbing fixture blocks or import your own DWG and DXF symbols. Compare symbol dimensions with manufacturer documentation.

Check accessibility and documentation

Dimension clear spaces and distances between fixtures. Accessibility, ventilation and safety requirements vary by jurisdiction; consult applicable codes before construction.
