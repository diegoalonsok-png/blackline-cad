CAD2D3D · BLACKLINE

Online 2D and 3D CAD for architecture

CAD2D3D by Blackline CAD brings technical drafting and architectural modeling into a browser-based workspace for architects, students and design professionals, without a desktop installation.

Launch CAD2D3D

2D floor plans and technical drawings

Draw lines, polylines, walls and openings; organize layers, use object snaps and add dimensions. Architectural blocks help populate drawings. See the command reference and 2D drafting guide.

Architectural 3D modeling

Explore walls, slabs, materials and cameras in the 3D workspace. Use plan and perspective views to inspect geometry. The modeling module is under development; evaluate available tools on a test model before relying on them for production.

Artistic rendering and architectural presentation

Turn a model view into a graphite, watercolor or pen illustration. See the online architectural rendering guide for styles, controls and export formats.

DXF and PDF workflow

Use DXF for drawing exchange and PDF for documentation. Remote DWG conversion is disabled; try the local browser converter for supported files. Verify drawing units, scale and entity compatibility before issuing documents.

Architectural exercise: from a 2D floor plan to a 3D volume

Start with a rectangular room measuring 4 × 5 m. Draft its boundaries in meters, separate walls from construction lines, and locate a door and a window. Use ArqAssist to search for CAD blocks by name. Check that furniture does not obstruct access or door swings.

In the 3D workspace, explore the room volume with an illustrative wall height of 2.70 m and a slab. Retain the floor plan as a dimensional reference and compare opening locations between both representations. Do not assume that every imported DXF entity can automatically become a solid: inspect the geometry and the tools supported by the modeling module.

Prepare a dimensioned floor plan and a presentation view. At a plot scale of 1:50, a 5 m wall occupies 100 mm on paper, but its model length must remain 5 m. Consult the DXF exchange guide and verify your PDF before printing.

How does CAD differ from a picture-based floor plan tool?

CAD represents geometric entities using coordinates and units, organizes them in layers, and allows precise editing. An image may provide a visual reference, but does not replace editable lines, polylines and blocks. CAD2D3D combines technical drafting, architectural resources and a 3D modeling workspace. It does not replace structural calculations or regulatory review.

Is there a free version?

Core drafting remains free. Pro tools are subject to the current plan and terms displayed in the editor.
