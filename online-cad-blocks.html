CAD2D3D · BLACKLINE

Online CAD blocks for architecture

A block library saves repetitive drafting work in floor plans, elevations and sections. CAD2D3D combines a visual gallery with insertion tools in the same drawing workspace.

Launch CAD2D3D

Use the visual gallery

Launch the editor, open the block gallery, and filter by category. Choose a thumbnail, then place the symbol in your drawing. Verify the dimensions before repeating the block.

Organize architectural symbols

Separate furniture, vegetation, plumbing fixtures, openings and annotation symbols. Match plan or elevation symbols to the drawing view. Retain source files and check their usage rights.

Bring your own library

The importer accepts ZIP, DWG and DXF files. Local conversion can reject unsupported geometry or drawings with missing units. Accepted blocks are stored in your browser, not automatically published.
