Bedroom and hotel room floor plans in CAD

Bedroom design coordinates dimensions, daylight, openings, storage and circulation. A scaled drawing helps compare housing and hospitality layouts.

Launch CAD2D3D

Draft the room

Draw walls and openings and mark access routes. Choose a documentation scale while keeping the model at real-world size.

Add furniture

Insert available bed, table and wardrobe blocks. Check dimensions and usable clearance. For an en-suite layout, coordinate the closet and bathroom with the room.

Repeat a room type

For hotel studies, repeat a tested room layout and adjust access, services and dimensions. Review the specific requirements of each room.

Worked example: a double bedroom

Use an illustrative internal room of 3.60 × 4.20 m. Draw the door and window first, then insert a bed measuring 1.60 × 2.00 m and a wardrobe using the intended manufacturer's dimensions. Use separate layers for furniture and circulation zones. Compare two bed orientations by rotating or copying furniture in a duplicate of the plan, not by modifying the original room envelope.

Measure the clear space beside the bed, the approach to the wardrobe and the path from the door to the window. Check whether wardrobe doors and drawers can open without conflicts. For hotel room types, preserve the reference layout as a separate project or reusable block, and adapt each room to its actual structural and service constraints. The example dimensions are design inputs, not a guarantee of regulatory compliance.
