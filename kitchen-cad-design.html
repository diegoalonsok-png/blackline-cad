Worked example: comparing two kitchen layouts

Use an illustrative 3.00 × 3.60 m interior and mark the door, window and existing service connections. Create independent layers for walls, base units, wall units and appliances. A preliminary 0.60 m cabinet depth can help establish the layout, but substitute the actual manufacturer dimensions before specifying products.

Test a linear arrangement and an L-shaped arrangement in separate copies of the drawing. Compare the route between the refrigerator, sink and cooking area, available preparation surfaces and drawer or appliance door conflicts. Dimension the clear aisle between cabinet fronts and check corner access. Before issuing drawings, verify connections, ventilation, product specifications and applicable local requirements. The exercise dimensions are illustrative, not regulatory minimums.
